# Editorial principles

## Curated, not exhaustive

The site should help a person decide what to do next. More links are not automatically more useful.

For each topic, aim for a very small default set:

- one good starting point;
- one useful alternative when the trade-off is meaningfully different;
- optionally one deeper resource.

## Organize around the job-search journey

The primary navigation follows the user's problem, not content format:

1. Define your search
2. Prepare your materials
3. Find opportunities
4. Evaluate and triage
5. Apply and track
6. Prepare for interviews
7. Offers and close

Articles, videos, tools, services, and communities are resource types inside these stages.

## AI-assisted curation

AI can discover candidate resources, summarize them, detect broken links, compare overlap, and draft metadata.

Publication remains an editorial decision. Generated summaries should be checked against the source before being marked reviewed.

## Commercial content

Sponsored or affiliate resources may exist later, but the relationship must be explicit in the resource metadata and visible to the reader. Commercial placement does not automatically qualify a resource for the curated set.
